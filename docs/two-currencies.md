# PokéBonk: ₽ vs Stardust

Summary of the unofficial [economy guide](https://pokebonk.info/guides/economy/) on [pokebonk.info](https://pokebonk.info/) (fan wiki; the game is at pokebonk.com).

- **₽** is spent inside a run: Poké Balls and Meowth's shop.
- **Stardust** is permanent and buys Lab upgrades.
- Read all three Poké Ball choices, sell items you will not use, and avoid repeated restocks.

Related: [level-up choices](https://pokebonk.info/guides/upgrades/).

Maintained by a pokebonk.info editor (unofficial).
