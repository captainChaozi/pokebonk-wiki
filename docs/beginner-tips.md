# Beginner tips

Condensed from the [PokéBonk Wiki](https://pokebonk.info/) beginner guide. Full page: [15 PokéBonk beginner tips](https://pokebonk.info/guides/beginner-tips/).

**TL;DR:** keep moving, read every card, use map markers, and spend early Stardust on damage and HP.

## Movement
- Never stand still (except with Metal Powder).
- Hold Tab to check the map without pausing.
- Jump over boss Shockwaves.
- In Survival, lead the horde over hostile ground.
- Once comfortable, Dive off ledges for area damage.

## Cards and moves
- Read the **Affects** line before picking a card.
- Spread your three moves over different ranges.
- Learn one partner reaction (e.g. Wet + Electric).
- Turn on Auto Ultimate if you forget to fire it.

## Economy
- Pick up the Pokémon Egg; it hatches into a Support Alpha after enough KOs.
- Charge every Unown altar.
- Mark Poké Balls and Meowth on the map.
- Sell unused items; Legendaries are worth about 1.5 Poké Balls.
- Avoid repeated Meowth restocks.
- Claim daily/weekly Tasks and achievements.
