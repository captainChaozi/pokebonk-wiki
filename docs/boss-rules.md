# Shared boss rules (Season 2)

Notes from the [Poke Bonk wiki bosses hub](https://pokebonk.info/guides/bosses/). Poke Bonk is the free browser game at pokebonk.com; this wiki is an independent fan resource.

| Rule | What it means |
|---|---|
| No one-shots | A single hit caps at about one third of your HP, then you are Wounded (half healing for 4 s) |
| Adaptive HP | Boss HP adapts, with a Season 2 Endure floor |
| Exhaustion | If the fight drags, you take double damage |
| Impatience | Triggers if you deal no damage for 12 s |
| Crowd control | Freeze/sleep/stun break after ~2 s; the boss ignores them for 5 s after |
| Telegraphs | Telegraphed hits always deal a minimum after armour |
| Fury | At low HP, faster and chained signature attacks |

Region legendaries with unique counters:

- [Zapdos](https://pokebonk.info/guides/bosses/zapdos/) on [Storm Coast](https://pokebonk.info/guides/maps/storm-coast/): stand by lightning rods; three charges knock it down.
- [Articuno](https://pokebonk.info/guides/bosses/articuno/) on [Celestial Glacier](https://pokebonk.info/guides/maps/celestial-glacier/).

Values marked pending in the wiki still need first-hand confirmation.
