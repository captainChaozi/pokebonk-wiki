# PokéBonk Stardust rewards: before and after Season 2 (fan wiki)

Source: [PokéBonk economy guide](https://pokebonk.info/guides/economy/) · Spend it: [Lab upgrades](https://pokebonk.info/guides/lab/) · Wiki: [PokéBonk Wiki](https://pokebonk.info/)

Stardust is the permanent currency, spent on Lab upgrades, item unlocks and clan slots. ₽ is spent inside a run.

## Reward table

| Source | Before Season 2 | Season 2 |
|---|---|---|
| Per zone cleared | 40 | 60 |
| Per boss | 35 | 60 |
| Per victory | 150 | 300 |
| Survival milestone | 30 | 45 |
| Survival legendary | 80 | 120 |
| KOs (cap per run) | 780 max | 780 max |

## What this means

- Victory rewards doubled, so a finished run is worth much more than an abandoned one.
- KO Stardust is capped at 780 per run. Zone, boss and victory rewards are separate from that cap.
- Tasks and achievements also pay Stardust.

The wiki tracked these figures on client 0.8.77. Check the in-game numbers after a new season.
