# PokéBonk troubleshooting quick card (unofficial)

Short version of the unofficial [BonkDex troubleshooting guide](https://pokebonk.info/guides/troubleshooting/) (updated 2026-10-31; client 0.8.76 as of 2026-10-29). The game is at pokebonk.com; this wiki is fan-made.

## Black screen or stuck loading

PokéBonk is 3D and needs **WebGL 2**.

- **Chrome / Edge / Opera:** Settings → System → turn on "Use hardware acceleration when available", restart the browser, update GPU drivers.
- **Firefox:** Settings → General → Performance → allow recommended settings / hardware acceleration.
- **Safari:** version 15 or newer; check WebGL 2.0 is not disabled under Develop → Experimental Features.
- If a "new version" prompt appears, reload. The client updates often.

## Lag or low FPS

Cap FPS and lower effects. A hot laptop usually means the browser is drawing as fast as it can.

## Keys do nothing

Click inside the game window, then switch your keyboard to **English input**. An IME (Chinese, Japanese, Korean input) can swallow WASD.

## Progress

Progress lives on your account. Guest progress is lost after 60 days unused.
