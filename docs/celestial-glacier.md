## Articuno in three lines

1. Keep a pillar between you and Articuno; the reflected Ice Beam freezes it for about 4.5 s.
2. In phases 2–3, break the 3 (then 4) lake crystals; while shelled it takes about 15% damage.
3. During Sheer Cold, move to a golden-edge sector.

## Season 2 altars here

Fossil Ruins, Rhydon's Roar (max 3 roars: +30% HP / +12% damage / +elite per roar, +Great Ball per roar on win), Porygon Altar, Bill's Machine.
