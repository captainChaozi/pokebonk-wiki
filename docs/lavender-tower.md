# Lavender Tower Night (Season 2 Halloween event)

Quick reference from the [BonkDex Lavender Tower guide](https://pokebonk.info/guides/lavender-tower/). Floor names, candle thresholds and achievements were cross-checked against public event panels and other write-ups; not every tier was claimed in-client. Re-verify in the live game if a number conflicts.

**Ends: 2026-11-04 17:30 UTC** (2026-11-05 01:30 UTC+8). After that the mode, event track and Halloween leaderboard stop.

## Seven floors (3 waves each, boss on wave 3)

| Floor | Name | Note |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Entrance Hall | Learn candelabra lighting |
| 2 | Hall of Tombstones | Trick-or-treat at Gengar's tombstones |
| 3 | Hall of Mist | Low visibility; keep candelabras lit |
| 4 | Snuffed Candles | Dark floor; *No Light* = clear without lighting |
| 5 | Purified Zone | Hold the sacred circle |
| 6 | Marowak's Shadow | Calm Ghost Marowak with three memorial candles |
| 7 | The Summit | Gengar, Lord of the Tower |

Then endless floors through **The Gate to the Beyond** for the event leaderboard.
